Research Interests

My primary research focuses on many-body quantum theory and algebraic quantum field theory.

I aim for a mathematically rigorous treatment of physical problems using functional analysis, $C^*$-algebras and their representations, as well as spectral theory.

More specifically, my current work involves studying differential equations on spaces of operators and applying these techniques to complex many-body quantum problems.
